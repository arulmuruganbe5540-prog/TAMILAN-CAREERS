TAMILAN CAREERS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we may store your name, email address and account type, as well as resumes, education and work experience details, and application information that you choose to provide.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to manage accounts, help users find matching jobs, share applications with employers, improve security and operate the service.

3. Information Sharing

When you apply for a job, information related to your application may be shared with the employer that posted the job. Your personal information is not sold, except where disclosure is required by law.

4. Document Security

Documents such as resumes and certificates should be stored with appropriate access controls. Do not share public links.

5. Your Choices

You may use the website's contact channel to request correction or deletion of your account information. Some records may be retained for legal or security reasons.

6. Third-Party Services

Authentication Third-party providers may be used for services such as hosting. Their privacy terms also apply.

An AI resume-matching feature that sends resume information to OpenAI or another AI provider is not currently enabled. The current resume-to-job matching feature uses local rule-based matching only. If an AI service is enabled in the future, users will be informed in advance about what information is shared with which provider, and it will be enabled only after explicit user consent.

8. Advertising, Cookies and Google AdSense

If Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled on this website, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads and measure advertising performance. These technologies may process browser or device information, IP addresses, advertising interactions and website usage information. Available advertising choices depend on your region and the controls provided by Google.

Learn more about Google advertising technologies and Google Privacy Policy pages for more information.

Where legally required, an appropriate consent management solution will be used before enabling advertising cookies or personalised advertising. You can also manage available choices through your browser settings or Google ad settings.

9. IP-Based Country Detection

The homepage may use a third-party IP lookup service to obtain your country code and prioritise relevant job opportunities. This feature does not use GPS or precise location. The third-party service's privacy practices also apply.

10. Contact and Requests

For privacy questions or requests to correct or delete information, contact us through Help Center · Cookie Policy contact us through the relevant page.

11. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as the service evolves.
