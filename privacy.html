Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TAMILAN CAREERS handles information when visitors browse the website, create accounts, build profiles, upload documents, search for jobs or submit applications.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include your name, email address, account type, profile details, education, skills, work experience, resume files, certificates, saved-job preferences, notifications and job application information you choose to provide.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate accounts, display relevant job opportunities, support job searches, manage applications, provide employer and candidate dashboards, protect the service, respond to support requests and maintain platform security.

3. When Information Is Shared

When you apply for a vacancy, the information and documents selected for that application may be made available to the employer responsible for the vacancy. Company users receive candidate information only through the platform's authorized employer workflows. Do not upload information you are not comfortable sharing for recruitment purposes.

Personal information is not sold by TAMILAN CAREERS. Information may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the service, its users or the public.

4. Accounts, Resumes and Document Security

Account access is managed through the platform's authentication provider. Resume and verification documents should be accessed only through authorized workflows. Never share passwords, one-time codes, private document links or authentication tokens. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices and Requests

You may request correction or deletion of account information by contacting arulmuruganbe5540@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request and may retain limited records where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

6. Service Providers

TAMILAN CAREERS uses Supabase for services such as authentication, database and file-related platform functions. The website also loads third-party resources described below. Those providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms.

7. Regional Context and Time Zone

The shared site experience may request country, region and time-zone information from the third-party service ipapi.co. This request is made by your browser; as with normal internet requests, the service can receive your IP address and technical request information. The result is used to display regional context and local time. The homepage may also use the country code to prioritize relevant job opportunities.

This feature does not use GPS or precise device location. The website interface remains in English; country detection does not translate or change the site language. If the lookup is unavailable, the site falls back to your device time zone and a general regional display.

8. Resume Matching and AI

The current resume-to-job matching experience uses rule-based matching against profile skills. An AI feature that sends resume or profile information to an external AI provider is not enabled for public use. If that changes, the information shared, provider and user controls will be explained before the feature is activated.

The Google AdSense publisher script is configured on the website. Whether advertisements are displayed may depend on publisher-account settings and Google's eligibility checks. Google and its partners may process IP address, browser or device information, cookies, advertising interactions and usage information to provide, measure or personalize advertising. See Google Advertising Technologies and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising choices and consent requirements depend on your country or region. The site operator must maintain any required certified consent-management configuration before personalized advertising is enabled in regions where it is mandatory. You can also review available controls in Google's ad settings and your browser.

10. Cookies and Browser Storage

The website and its service providers may use browser storage or cookies for authentication sessions, security, theme preferences and third-party services. The theme preference is stored locally in your browser. See the Cookie Policy for more detail.

This policy may be updated when the website or its providers change. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact arulmuruganbe5540@gmail.com.
