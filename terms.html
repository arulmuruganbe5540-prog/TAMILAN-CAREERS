Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Purpose of the Service

TAMILAN CAREERS helps job seekers and employers share and discover employment information. We do not guarantee that users will receive a job offer.

2. User Responsibilities

Users must provide accurate information. Fake job postings, fraudulent employers, misleading information, illegal content and activities that violate others' rights are not permitted.

3. Employment Verification

Before applying, independently verify the employer's official website, contact details and job terms. Be cautious of anyone asking you to pay money to get a job.

4. Account Security

Keep your login credentials confidential. Change your password if you notice suspicious activity on your account.
