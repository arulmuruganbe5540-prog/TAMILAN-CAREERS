Which State Government Jobs Can You Search For?

In many states, the following departments recruit through the PSC or separate departmental recruitment boards.

🏛️ Administrative RolesGroup A/B/C, Secretariat, Assistant, Clerk
👮 PolicePolice Constable, SI, Jail, Fire & Rescue
📚 Education DepartmentTeacher, Lecturer, Professor, Education Offices
🏥 HealthcareNurse, Pharmacist, Lab Technician, Medical Officer
🌳 Forest DepartmentForest Guard, Forester, Range Officer
⚡ Electricity & TransportElectricity Board, Transport, Technical posts
⚖️ JudiciaryHigh Court, District Court, Court Assistant
🌾 Other Government DepartmentsAgriculture, Revenue, Rural Development, Local Bodies
Not all state department jobs are recruited through the PSC. Also check separate notifications from the Police Recruitment Board, Staff Selection Board, Teacher Recruitment Board, Health Department, High Court, Electricity Board and government organisations on their respective official websites.

How to Apply for State Government Jobs

1. Select a StateSelect your preferred state.
2. Read the NotificationCheck education, age, language and residency requirements.
3. Apply OfficiallyRegister and apply on the official government website.
4. Check StatusHall Ticket, Check examination dates and results.
Before applying for jobs in another state, carefully review domicile or local residency rules, state-language requirements, reservation rules and certificate requirements. Do not assume that candidates from other states are eligible for every job.
