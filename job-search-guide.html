How to Search for Jobs – Complete Guide

Learn step by step, from choosing the right job and applying to preparing a resume, attending interviews and avoiding job scams.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Plan Before You Search

First, list your education, experience, skills, preferred job type and preferred work location. Rather than applying for every job, focus on opportunities that match your qualifications.

  • Education: 10th Pass, 12th Pass, ITI, Diploma, Degree or postgraduate education.
  • Skills: computers, languages, technical work, machine operation, sales or accounting.
  • Location: your city, nearby district, state or another country.
  • Job type: full-time, part-time, internship or contract.

Search for skills as well as job titles. Keywords such as “Data Entry”, “Excel” and “Customer Support” can help.

2. Prepare a Clear Resume

Keep your resume concise and easy to read, ideally two to three pages. Freshers should highlight education, training, projects and skills. Experienced candidates should include responsibilities and measurable achievements.

  • Name, professional email, contact number and city.
  • A concise career objective or profile summary.
  • Education details – qualification, institution and year of completion.
  • Skills, certificates, training and work experience.
  • Projects or achievements relevant to the job.

Do not include false experience or fake certificates. Update your resume to highlight the skills relevant to each job.

3. Verify Government Job Notifications

Before applying for a government job, read the notification on the official recruiting authority's website. Summaries on job portals are for guidance only; the official notification is the final source.

  • Notification number, job title and number of vacancies.
  • Educational qualifications, age limits and reservation rules.
  • Application fee and official payment method.
  • Application start date, deadline and selection process.
  • Official website URL and notification PDF.

Apply early instead of waiting until the deadline. Keep your application receipt and registration number safe.

4. Applying for Private-Sector Jobs

Verify the company's name, official website, office address, industry and contact email. Check that the job description clearly states responsibilities, salary range, working hours, location and required experience.

For overseas jobs, verify the visa type, work permit, contract, salary, accommodation, insurance and recruitment licence through the relevant government authorities. Do not accept offers that require you to work on a tourist visa.

5. Avoid Job Scams

Important Safety Note: Be extremely cautious if anyone asks you to send money to a personal UPI ID in exchange for a job guarantee or offer letter.
  • Never share your OTP, bank PIN, password or full card details.
  • Verify unrealistic promises such as very high salaries, no experience required or guaranteed immediate employment.
  • Independently verify the company's official domain email and phone number.
  • Do not install suspicious links, APK files or remote-access apps.
  • Check that the company name, position, salary, location and terms are correct in the offer letter.

If you experience cyber fraud in India, contact your bank immediately and report it using helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.

6. Prepare for Interviews

  • Research the company and job description in advance.
  • Practise introducing your education, experience and skills in one to two minutes.
  • Practise common job-related questions and practical skills.
  • For online interviews, prepare a reliable internet connection, camera, audio and a quiet location.
  • Ask clearly about salary, working hours, leave, work location and contract terms.
