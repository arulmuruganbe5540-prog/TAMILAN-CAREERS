Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This page explains how TAMILAN CAREERS and its service providers may use cookies and similar browser storage technologies.

1. Essential Authentication and Security

Authentication and account features may use browser storage or cookies to maintain a secure session and support functions you request. Blocking essential storage may prevent login, profile, application or other account features from working.

2. Theme Preference

The website stores your selected light or dark theme preference in your browser's local storage so that the preference can be remembered on later visits. This preference is not used to change the language of the website.

3. Regional Context and Time Zone

The shared site experience may contact ipapi.co to obtain country, region and time-zone information for the regional information bar and local clock. The request is made by your browser and the provider can receive your IP address and technical request information. The homepage may use the country code to prioritize relevant job opportunities. The website does not use GPS or precise device location and remains English-only for every visitor.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

The Google AdSense publisher script is configured on the website. Whether advertisements are displayed may depend on publisher-account settings and Google's eligibility checks. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process IP address, browser or device information, advertising interactions and usage information to provide, measure or personalize advertising.

Available advertising choices depend on your country or region. The site operator must maintain any required certified consent-management configuration before personalized advertising is enabled in regions where it is mandatory. For details, see Google Advertising Technologies and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

Supabase provides authentication and other platform services. Third-party providers may process technical information when your browser connects to their services. Their own privacy and cookie terms also apply.

6. Managing Browser Storage

You can review, delete or block cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect sign-in and other requested website features. Browser controls do not necessarily stop all information sent through ordinary network requests.
