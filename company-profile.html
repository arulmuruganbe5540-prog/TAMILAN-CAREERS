🏢 Company Registration & Official Verification

Register your company and submit legal documents. Your company becomes verified only after admin review. Once verified, authorised members can publish eligible private-sector jobs.

Important Security: Submitting documents does not automatically grant verified status. An administrator independently checks the relevant official registry in the company's country. Government registry integration is not currently automated; verification is performed manually.
