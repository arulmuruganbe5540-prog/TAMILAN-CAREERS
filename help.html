Help Center

Basic guidance for accounts, job search and safety.

1. How do I create a new account?

Go to the Login page, choose Job Seeker or Company, and register. Complete email confirmation if requested.

2. How do I search for jobs?

Use the Find Jobs page to search by title, skills, job type and location. Confirm vacancy details with the employer.

3. What should I do if I forget my password?

On the Login page Password Select “Forgot Password?” and enter your registered email address. Use the secure link sent to you to set a new password.

4. How do I update my profile?

Login Log in and open the profile link in your Dashboard to update your education, skills, experience and preferences.

5. Where can I find government job notifications?

HomeUse the Indian Government Jobs and Tamil Nadu Government Jobs sections on the relevant pages. Verify final details in the official notification.

6. Do I need to pay money to apply for a job?

Do not pay individuals who promise to secure a job or share your OTP, bank PIN or password. Independently verify the employer's official contact details.

7. Does this website show Google advertisements?

இந்த இணையதளத்தின் சில பக்கங்களில் Google AdSense An advertising code is configured. Whether ads appear depends on Google account approval, site eligibility, ad settings and Google's automated selection. Ad placements are maintained so they do not obscure content.

8. What if a job posting contains an error?

Compare the details with the official notice or company website. Report incorrect or suspicious information to us, and do not pay money based on unverified information.

9. Where can I find cookie and privacy information?

Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy pages for more information.

Note: This help page provides general guidance. Do not assume that features not yet integrated into the website are operational.
