How to Apply for Government Jobs?

A step-by-step guide from reading the notification to safely keeping your application receipt.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In This Guide

1. Find the Official Notification First

Do not rely only on social media posts, WhatsApp messages or third-party websites. Check whether the same notification appears on the official website of the relevant government department or recruiting authority.

  • Name of the organisation and its official website domain.
  • Notification number, publication date and job title.
  • Official PDF notification and any corrigenda.
  • Does the application link lead to the same official website?

A revised notification may have been published later. Check the latest version on the day you apply.

2. Check Eligibility and Age Limits

  • Educational qualifications, subject requirements, marks or equivalent qualifications.
  • The specified date used to calculate age.
  • Age relaxations for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PwBD and ex-servicemen, according to the notification.
  • Experience, language skills, physical eligibility or technical certificate requirements.
  • Rules on whether one person may apply for multiple posts.

If you are not eligible, do not apply using incorrect information. If an equivalency certificate or eligibility approval is required, read the relevant conditions.

3. Prepare Your Documents in Advance

  • Photograph and signature in the size and file format specified in the notification.
  • Educational certificates, mark sheets and proof of date of birth.
  • Caste, income, EWS or disability certificates, if applicable and valid.
  • Identity document and active email address / mobile number.
  • Experience certificate or technical licence, if required for the post.

Scan files clearly and save them in the size and format specified on the official website. Do not send your documents to unknown people.

4. Complete the Online Application Carefully

  1. Create an account only on the official website.
  2. Enter your name, date of birth, address and education details exactly as shown on your certificates.
  3. Upload your photograph, signature and required documents.
  4. If a fee is required, pay only through the official application portal.
  5. Review all details on the preview page before selecting Final Submit.
  6. Save your application number, PDF copy, payment receipt and confirmation email.
Warning: Never share your OTP, password, UPI PIN or bank details. Do not send application fees to an individual's bank account.

5. What Should You Do After Applying?

  • Check the application status and whether a correction window is available on the official website.
  • Check the hall ticket release date and examination centre details.
  • Review the exam timetable, syllabus and official sample papers.
  • Continue checking for answer keys, objection windows, results, merit lists and document verification notices.
  • Keep the notification PDF for each stage and a copy of your application.

6. Identify Fake Government Job Notifications

Do not trust anyone who asks for money in exchange for a guaranteed job, direct appointment or a job without an examination. Government recruitment procedures, fees and selection methods are stated in the relevant official notification.

  • Spelling errors, fake domains, personal email addresses or suspicious URLs.
  • Job notifications without a notification number.
  • Requests to send money to a personal UPI ID or bank account.
  • Requests for OTPs, Aadhaar OTP, passwords or remote-access apps.

If you experience online fraud in India, contact your bank immediately and report it through 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
